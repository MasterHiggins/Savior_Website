1.3.0 Release Date

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article does not contain spoilers

Let's just get to the brass tacks.

Savior's 1.3.0 update, the second last one, will be released August 21st, 2026. The one after that, 2.0.0, is the final one.

Despite this update being finished, the release is a bit far to spread things out evenly. I'm still deep into work on 2.0.0, and it's taking a while. The release won't be until late September, early October.

This was a very different presentation than the others, but now that Savior is getting closer to being finished, it's getting to this point.

The story is now reaching its climax, and it's getting to a point where morality can be questioned. One of the playtesters have a very interesting theory, however I'll leave that for everyone else to decide.

But yeah, 1.3.0 is only a small amount of time away. That's great.

Next, I'd like to provide an update on the final update, 2.0.0, the one after this one the article is on.

As of typing, which is July 29th, 2026, I'm working on the final boss of Savior. Wow, that's crazy to type. The final boss. Wow.

By the time this article comes out, it will have likely been completed. And, the epilogue is being worked on. 2.0.0 is an absolutely huge update, but try not to look too far ahead for now. Let's focus on getting there first, and it's a hard road to cross through.

1.3.0 is a bigger area than anything in the game so far. Even bigger than the island. Bigger than the forest. It's just huge. And, dangerous.

Lastly, I'd like to touch on the soundtrack of the video.

I made this track specifically for the clips used today, like I did before, so don't expect it to appear ingame. However, there is an actual name for this one.

Cosmic Destruction. It's styled very closely to the later tracks in the game. Infact, an early track for 1.3.0 was also close to this, but it SUCKED so I didn't use it. Anyway, this is a good glimpse into what to expect for the rest of the game, specially in 2.0.0

It's hard to think that Savior is almost finished. By this time next month, 2.0.0 will probably be ready to go with heavy testing already underway.
