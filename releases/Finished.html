Game Over

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article does not contain spoilers

Savior, after 14,648 blocks (+7,450), is finished.

As of this evening (August 5th, 2026), I finished the final update, and therefore the game.

You'll soon get a release date for the final update. Because of the sheer size of it, I decided not to include any side features this time around.

I'm not taking any breaks, and I've already begun planning out the next project. It will be taking place in the Savior universe, but it will feature a completely different main character and tells an entirely different story that ties into Savior. I can't wait to share more about this game that's been cooking in my head since January.

Just wanted to share the news.
