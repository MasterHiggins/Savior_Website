2.0.0 Releasing Soon

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article does not contain spoilers

Hello my star citizens.

Savior 2.0.0 is releasing in less than two weeks. This story will come together in a great way, but without knowing all the pieces. A lot is left up to interpretation. Someone who tested the full game said they expected the ending to be drastically different based on how it was being set up and that's great! Savior is meant to have many different ideas without the context of what happens on the cosmic scale.

There won't be any patch notes for 2.0.0, and there isn't a 2.0.1 patch planned or anything. When the game releases, that's it.

Sometime after Savior releases, I intend to do a full dev playthrough. I won't be playing a special build or anything, just a normal run through of the game and giving some development insights.

This is likely the last article before the update drops. After that, I'll talk a little more about what's happening next.
