Preparing For Final Updates

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article does not contain major spoilers

Hey everyone. Today, I have a somewhat important article to write, as it involves the game's difficulty. Some testers are finding updates 1.2.0, and especially 1.3.0, very difficult for some.

As such, there are some things I recommend doing on a playthrough that can help with this.

First of all, try to upgrade your weapon when you get to every Ancient Module. Once you reach that point, there should be enough material to level up. You can level up seven times, so get to that, and it's a good start. You can beat the game without it, but more damage and reach is a really good thing.

Next is all farming related. I would suggest getting all five hearts that you can, as well as the extra damage upgrade. That's a great way to get ahead.

That's a really good point for the next update, and it can honesty get you through it. For the updates after, I suggest exploring. There are NPCs that can really help out, especially one in particular.

At that point, it's just a matter of finishing the game. The last area is a gauntlet, it's going to be hard, but you'll get through it. Thank you.
