What is Update 1.3.0

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article may contain spoilers

1.3.0 is almost here, and I want to quickly highlight the features.

Feature 1: Side Dungeon Path

The dungeons now have one extra room. It's difficult, and intended for near the end of the game. You can go through it normally, but I... wouldn't.

Feature 2: Doubled Farm Perks

When attaining maxed farm perks, and wasting 10,000 materials, certain perks are now doubled. You get this for free upon criteria being met and interaction.

Main Event: Sterzonia

This is a two for one area, but shockingly, it is the second largest in the game, falling behind due to the insanity of 2.0.0's entire world. This episode will change a lot of perspectives. When I had someone to test it, they questioned the morality of Alex. However, I think it's best to draw your own conclusions.

The Next Update, 2.0.0

I don't want to say much.

The best area is in this update, the best songs are, the story is completed here, it really is my favourite.

There is a planned 1.3.1 bug fix, and it will go out a few days after the update releases.

The update is out on Friday, I hope you will like it!

Update Timing

I want to very quickly mention the time this update is releasing. I cannot schedule them and have to manually release updates. Usually, I do so the night before it comes out, according to my time zone. This time, it will be the afternoon before, so almost the entire world will get it a day early.

That's all. 2.0.0 is around the corner. I will see you soon!
