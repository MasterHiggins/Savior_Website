2.0.0 Development Update

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
This article does not contain spoilers

Hello everyone.

Even though Savior 1.3.0 isn't releasing for a few more weeks, I wanted to provide an update on the one after, the final up. 2.0.0, the big one. The finale.

As of August 1st, 2026, the final boss and sequences were finished and ready for testing. This is also the entirety of that area too.

And now, I'm working on the epilogue. Development is going well, but it's not fun yet to play, so that needs to be changed. While it's not terribly important, it should still be a fun, quiet end to the game.

I'm thinking that the epilogue will be fleshed out in a few days with a very clear path. Then, development will be fast. After that, it's just testing.

All in all, 2.0.0 will definitely be finished before 1.3.0 releases. Then, it's scheduling.

I'd say that 2.0.0 will be released in September should this rate continue. But again, it's hard to make the epilogue fun with the current limitations of the engine. It is really, really hard to develop things without some of the features, and it isn't worth sinking a ton of time into those features needed for the epilogue.

So, I decided to take a more Animal Crossing approach with it. I don't want to spoil anything, so you'll see what I mean when it releases.

It's worth noting I'm typing this on August 4th, 2026. By the time this article comes out Thursday or Friday, things may have changed incredibly.

As a side note, I've been looking into Steam Achievements.

I doubt they'll be implemented at this current point in time, but it is something I'm looking at.

But on the other hand, ingame achievements are kind of being built now anyway. We'll see what happens.

I'm still working on Mac and Linux ports. I'm not sure when they will be out yet, but they are coming. Soon. Hopefully. That's my goal for the end of the year. The Mac port is... slow. But the Linux port is going well. That could be out in just a few months.

And lastly, thank you. The next article will be 1.3.0's release, and the one after that is the first real article for 2.0.0, and I can't wait.
