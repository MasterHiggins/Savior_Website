Update 1.2.0 Progress

Article from Chris Higgins
(Developer)
THIS ARTICLE CONTAINS SPOILERS FOR THE GAME UP TO NOW

Hello everyone.

You may remember that I've said before that the next progress update will be a big one. This is a big one.

As of ten minutes ago (from me typing this), 1.2.0 is considered feature complete. That's crazy to even think, because the update started work not even a month ago.

At this time, I'm preparing a build for release after some bugs have been fixed. Despite the shorter time, this update is generally bigger than the last one. If you look at the size of areas, by their rooms, this one is somewhere in between the Mesa on the high end and Grasslands, I think. The area serves its purpose, and when it's done, it's done. It didn't overstay its welcome like some areas may have in the past.

This doesn't mean much really, but the patch notes have since been finished as well, and I have made preparations for the presentation for this update.

So, when is this update releasing? I want at least of of the next one finished when this update comes out. My goal is end of July, but it could be sooner. I do have an internal date this time, but it is a short wait.

Currently, I have no intention to release a 1.2.1 patch, but if there are huge problems, then there will be.

That's all for this progress update. Thank you for reading.
